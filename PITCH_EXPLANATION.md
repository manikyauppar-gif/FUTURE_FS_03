---

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Problem
Urban Brew Café is a popular local coffee shop in Bengaluru known for artisanal espresso and fresh bakery items. However, the business relied solely on foot traffic and third-party food aggregator apps, facing the following critical challenges:

1. **Loss of High-Margin Direct Table Bookings:** Customers could not reserve tables in advance, leading to long weekend wait times and lost group bookings.
2. **Third-Party Commission Fees:** High commission fees paid to delivery platforms cut into profits on takeaway orders.
3. **Outdated Paper Menus:** Inability to update daily special bakery items in real time.

---

## 2. Digital Solution Architecture
To solve these business challenges, a modern digital web solution was engineered featuring:

- **Interactive Digital Menu:** Customers can view categorized menu items (Coffee, Bakery, All-Day Breakfast) directly on their mobile phones.
- **Instant Table Reservation Engine:** Frictionless online table booking modal to capture customer inquiries and reduce weekend table bounce rates.
- **Local SEO & Location Discovery Integration:** Prominently displays operating hours, live open/close status, and location details.

---

## 3. Projected Business Impact & Return on Investment (ROI)
- **35% Increase in Weekend Bookings:** Pre-booked table reservations ensure higher seat utilization.
- **Enhanced Customer Loyalty:** Digital menu access improves customer experience and repeat visits.
- **Direct Order Inquiries:** Bypasses third-party app commissions for catering and private group reservations.
